METRIC MIRAGE UPGRADE: CSV / Excel upload
==========================================

HOW TO INSTALL
1. Close the running app (Ctrl+C in the terminal).
2. Unzip this archive. Copy the FOLDERS inside metric_mirage_upgrade/ into your project folder
   (the one that contains app.py). Let your system MERGE the folders and REPLACE files when asked.
   Your other files (the analysis engines in core/, the sql/ and reports/ folders, data/raw,
   data/processed, requirements.txt) are NOT touched: nothing in them needed changing.
3. In the project folder, with your virtual environment active:

       pytest -v
       streamlit run app.py

4. In the app, open "Upload Data" in the sidebar and press "Try the sample file (simulated)".
